Agricultural Pathology AI

Wheat Leaf Disease Detection System

Wheat is a critical staple crop supporting global food security. Leaf diseases cause extensive yield degradation if unaddressed. This deep learning diagnostic system utilizes ResNet50 neural representations to deliver rapid, reliable disease classification.

Open Diagnostic Tool
Recognized Disease Pathologies

Fusarium Head Blight

Also known as head scab, causing premature bleaching of spikelets and sterile or shriveled grain development.

Healthy Wheat

Normal vegetative foliage exhibiting uniform chlorophyll pigmentation without necrotic lesions or spotting.

Leaf Rust

Caused by Puccinia triticina, presenting as small, circular, reddish-orange uredinial pustules on leaf blades.

Tan Spot

Fungal infection caused by Pyrenophora tritici-repentis, developing tan lesions with distinct chlorotic yellow borders.

About This Diagnostic Tool

This application was developed as a Data Warehousing and Mining Project-Based Learning (PBL) solution to facilitate rapid crop health assessment for agronomists and farmers.

The core machine learning engine uses transfer learning with a ResNet50 convolutional backbone fine-tuned to classify wheat leaf specimens into Fusarium Head Blight, Leaf Rust, Tan Spot, and Healthy classes with high precision.
